How to play

You run Team 3, a chip business competing with 7 rival teams over 4 rounds. The team with the highest cumulative profit wins.

  1. Scan: compare six markets on demand, price pressure and rival activity. The free signals are rough estimates, and conditions change every round.
  2. Develop: choose a chip family. Chips fit some markets far better than others. In rounds 1 and 3 you get a research point to upgrade your chip for your market.
  3. Compete: buy up to one report ($0.05M), then set your price, marketing budget and production run. Production costs $20 per chip, sold or not.
  4. Review: see revenue, costs, profit and how your prediction compared with real sales.

Market rule: moving to a different market from the previous round costs a $0.30M penalty, and your upgrade loses its value. Teams that stay in a market build loyalty. Marketing works best when you enter a new market.

Each round also costs a fixed $0.30M in development. Each team can sell at most 60,000 chips per round.
